A little studio for gentle notes

overleyz puts words on pictures. Choose how you feel, pick a painting, a travel photo or a picture of your own, add a note and a few stickers, and share it as an Instagram post or a short reel.

Make a post — it’s free

How it works

  1. Check in. Each day overleyz starts by asking How are you today? — pick a smiley. Your answer sets up words, a picture and music to match — and colours today on your little mood calendar.
  2. Say how you feel. Hopeful, heartbroken, grateful, in love, tired, brave, wanderlust, letting go, growing, or missing your people. overleyz suggests notes, pictures and stickers that match — or tap Surprise me.
  3. Choose a picture. 170+ text-free leyzays paintings, 250+ travel photos from 60 places, plain soft paper, or your own photo.
  4. Add your words. Write your own, or borrow one of 450+ leyzays notes or a classic line from a well-loved book or poem. Pick the lettering, ink and size, underline a phrase, and drag the words where you want them.
  5. Soft or Loud. Soft is the pastel studio with handwritten notes. Loud turns everything dark, with strong colours and huge capital letters that shout — underline a phrase and it shouts in a second colour.
  6. Add stickers. The Sugar Valley dessert friends, journal washi and doodles, seasonal stickers and little watercolour still-lifes. Drag them, resize and turn them.
  7. Share. Make a 4:5 post, a square, or a 9:16 story — or an 8-second reel where your words appear line by line, with music from the leyzays songs. On a phone, Share opens your apps: Instagram, Facebook, Messenger, Viber.
  8. Or send it as a note. Tap the envelope, add who it’s for, and send the link. When they open it, the picture appears, then your words — like opening a letter — and they can write one back.

The words

leyzays notes are written by Ley. Classic quotes come only from books, poems and scripture in the public domain — Shakespeare, Austen, the Brontës, Dickinson, Thoreau, Gibran, Montgomery, Alcott and more — with the author and work printed underneath. We leave out movie lines and quotes from living people: they’re protected, and too many are misattributed online.

Your privacy

  • Nothing is uploaded. overleyz runs entirely in your browser. Your photo, your words and the finished picture stay on your device until you choose to share them.
  • No accounts, no cookies, no ads. To see how overleyz is doing, we count anonymous totals — how many people visit each day, which moods are picked, how many posts are shared, and whether a visit came from TikTok or Instagram. No cookies, IP addresses or visitor IDs are kept, and nothing you write or make is ever sent. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, you aren’t counted at all.
  • Your mood calendar stays on your phone. The daily check-in is saved only in this browser — we never see it.
  • Notes live inside the link. A note you send is written into the link itself, not stored on our server. Anyone who has the link can open it, so send it only to the person it’s for. Your own photo never goes into a link.
  • Only your last design is remembered, in this browser, so you can pick up where you left off. Your own photo is never kept. Clear it any time with Start over.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Every picture carries a small #leyzays and overleyz.art mark in the corners — that’s the only ask.

Why doesn’t it post straight to Instagram?

Instagram doesn’t let websites post for you. On a phone, tap Share and choose Instagram — your picture or reel opens there ready to post. On a computer, save it and upload it.

My reel saved as WebM, not MP4.

Some browsers can only record WebM. Instagram prefers MP4 — Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android or desktop record MP4.

Where does the music come from?

Every tune is an original leyzays song, composed for the notes on leyzays — so your reels are safe to post.

Can I sell what I make?

Please don’t. The paintings, photos, characters and leyzays notes are © Ley Z and are shared here for personal posts only. If you’d like to use them for something else, ask first.

Can I use my own quote from a movie or a famous person?

You can type anything into the words box for your own personal post. Please credit the person you’re quoting.

Where can I see more?

Notes and printables at leyzays, travel on @traveleyz, and the Sugar Valley story at sugarvalley.
